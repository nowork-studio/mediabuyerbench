Top diagnosis: yes, you need fresh creative, but the bigger issue is creative fatigue inside a saturated, too-small audience.

Evidence:
- CPA doubled from `$25.61` to `$53.49`, calculated as spend / purchases.
- Spend was similar: `$2,100` previous 7d vs `$2,300` current 7d, but purchases fell from `82` to `43`.
- CTR fell from `1.8%` to `0.9%`, meaning clicks / impressions were cut in half.
- CVR stayed mostly stable: `3.4%` to `3.2%`, meaning purchases / clicks did not materially degrade.
- Frequency jumped from `1.9` to `5.8`, meaning impressions / reached users are getting repetitive.
- Delivery is concentrated: `UGC demo v3` is 39 days old, taking `72%` of spend, and now at `$58` CPA.

Smallest safe next actions:
1. Launch new creatives immediately, but as a controlled refresh, not a full rebuild.
   - Add 4-6 new variants: new hooks, new first 3 seconds, new UGC demos, objection handling, testimonial/proof angle, and benefit-led static/video.
   - Keep the best existing ads live while new assets gather signal.

2. Reduce reliance on the fatigued winner.
   - Do not let `UGC demo v3` continue taking 72% of spend if it is producing `$58` CPA.
   - Gradually shift budget toward new variants and the lower-CPA existing ads.

3. Expand the audience test.
   - A 450k interest stack with `5.8` frequency is likely saturated.
   - Test broader targeting or a larger stacked/broad ad set while keeping budget controlled.

What not to do yet:
- Do not overhaul the landing page; CVR is stable, so the main break is pre-click.
- Do not scale budget until CPA moves back near the `$28` target.
- Do not kill all old creatives at once; use them as controls.

Measure after launch:
- CTR: clicks / impressions, should recover first.
- CVR: purchases / clicks, should stay near 3.2-3.4%.
- CPA: spend / purchases, target is `$28`.
- Frequency: impressions / reach, should come down or stabilize.
- Spend share by creative: make sure new ads actually receive delivery.
- Creative-level CPA after each asset has spent at least about 1-2x target CAC.
