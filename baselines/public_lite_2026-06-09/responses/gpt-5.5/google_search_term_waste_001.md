Top diagnosis: broad match expansion is pulling in low/irrelevant intent and diluting conversion rate, not just causing a traffic volume issue.

Evidence:
- CPA worsened from `$59` to `$101`, above the `$65` target.
- Clicks increased from `410` to `515`, but conversions fell from `20` to `14`.
- Conversion rate dropped from `20 / 410 = 4.9%` to `14 / 515 = 2.7%`.
- In the current period, `dog boarding near me` performed well: `7 conversions / 86 clicks = 8.1% CVR`, `$310 / 7 = $44 CPA`.
- Bad broad-match terms spent `$527` with `203 clicks` and `0 conversions`: `cheap dog kennel seattle`, `dog boarding jobs`, `free dog sitting`, `cat boarding seattle`. That is `527 / 1420 = 37%` of spend with no qualified booking requests.

Smallest safe next actions:
1. Add negatives immediately:
   - `jobs`, `hiring`, `career`
   - `free`
   - `cheap` if the business does not want price-sensitive leads
   - `cat` / `cat boarding` if cat boarding is not offered or not a target service
2. Pull back the broad-match expansion that introduced these terms, or isolate broad match into its own capped test campaign/ad group.
3. Preserve what is working: keep coverage for high-intent dog boarding/daycare terms like `dog boarding near me`, `dog boarding seattle`, `dog daycare seattle`, using exact/phrase where possible.
4. Review search terms every few days this week and add negatives before spend accumulates.

Do not overhaul bidding yet. Maximize conversions is not the first suspect because the issue is clear query quality deterioration after broad match expansion.

Measure after changes:
- CPA on qualified booking requests
- Conversion rate: conversions / clicks
- Spend share on irrelevant or zero-conversion search terms
- Search-term CPA for core dog boarding/daycare queries
- Lead quality, not just form volume, since the target is qualified booking requests.
