## Top Diagnosis: Audience Saturation Collapsing CTR

This is **not primarily a creative quality problem — it's an audience exhaustion problem.** The evidence is unambiguous:

- **CTR halved: 1.8% → 0.9%** — people stopped clicking
- **CVR held flat: 3.4% → 3.2%** — people who do click still convert at the same rate
- **Frequency tripled: 1.9 → 5.8** — each of your ~450k users has seen the ads ~6 times on average

The CPA math follows directly: `CPA ≈ CPM / (CTR × CVR)`. CVR is stable, so if CTR halves, CPA roughly doubles. That's exactly what happened ($25.61 → $53.49). The landing page is exonerated by the flat CVR.

A 450k interest-stack audience at 5.8 frequency means the engaged segment is largely burned through. New creatives launched into the same saturated pool will fatigue faster than the current ones did — you'll buy yourself days, not weeks.

## Evidence Summary

| Signal | Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| CTR drop | 1.8% → 0.9% | Ad blindness / banner fatigue from overexposure |
| CVR stable | 3.4% → 3.2% | Offer and LP are fine |
| Frequency | 5.8 | Well past the 3.0–3.5 warning zone for cold audiences |
| All creatives 26–39 days old | High spend share | No fresh angles in rotation |
| UGC v3 at 72% spend share | CPA $58 | Algo over-concentrated; accelerated burn |

---

## Smallest Safe Next Actions (in priority order)

**1. Expand the audience — this is the highest-leverage lever.**
- Add 1–3% lookalike from purchaser list (keep separate from interest stack, don't merge)
- Test Advantage+ audience on a fresh campaign or ad set with a small budget ($150–200/day) to let Meta find incremental reach
- Do NOT blow up the existing ad set — it's out of learning and holds useful signal

**2. Launch new creatives simultaneously, but into expanded audience.**
- Test 2–3 meaningfully different hooks, not iterations. Different format (problem-first angle vs. demo), different spokesperson, or different emotion (anxiety vs. aspiration).
- UGC v3 at 72% spend share and 39 days old is the primary candidate to retire — but don't kill it until a replacement is performing
- Static benefit at $46 CPA is your current best performer; understand why and replicate the angle

**3. Redistribute budget away from UGC v3 gradually.**
- Reduce its budget share by ~30% (stay within ~20% single-day change to avoid re-triggering learning)
- Reallocate to Founder Story and Static Benefit to test their CPAs with more volume

---

## What Not to Do Yet

- **Don't increase overall spend** — you'll just burn more into a saturated audience at $53 CPA
- **Don't restructure campaigns** (duplicate, consolidate, change optimization event) — the ad set is out of learning and you'll restart the learning phase at a cost of ~50 events
- **Don't assume a new creative iteration solves this** — same audience + new ad = same frequency problem within 2 weeks

## What to Measure After the Change

| Metric | Target | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| CTR on new creatives | ≥1.5% | Days 3–5 |
| Frequency on expanded audience | ≤2.5 | Day 7 |
| CPA on lookalike/Advantage+ | ≤$35 | Day 7–10 |
| Blended account CPA | Trending toward $35 | Day 10 |
| CVR stability | Stays ≥3.0% | Ongoing — confirms LP isn't degrading |

If CTR recovers on the **same creatives** when shown to the new audience, you've confirmed the diagnosis: the content is fine, the pool is exhausted. If CTR stays low even on fresh audiences, then creative fatigue is a co-contributor and you'll need to go deeper on new angles.
