## Top diagnosis: Creative fatigue + audience saturation — not a conversion problem.

The CPA doubling is driven almost entirely by a **CTR collapse (1.8% → 0.9%, ~50% drop)** coinciding with **frequency tripling (1.9 → 5.8)**. CVR is essentially flat (3.4% → 3.2%, within noise). Since CPA ≈ CPM ÷ (CTR × CVR), halving CTR alone roughly doubles CPA — which is exactly what happened. The same people are seeing the same ads too many times and have stopped clicking.

**Concentration:** UGC demo v3 is 39 days old and absorbing **72% of spend** at a $58 CPA. The fatigue is largely one tired ad against a finite 450k audience, where frequency 5.8/week is the tell.

### Evidence & denominators
- **CTR** = clicks ÷ impressions → the broken metric.
- **CVR** = purchases ÷ clicks → stable, confirming the landing page/offer/audience-intent is fine (consistent with "no material LP change").
- **Frequency** 5.8 over 7d on ~450k = saturation, not a fluke.
- Caveat: current CPA rests on only **43 purchases** — noisy week-over-week — but CTR and frequency are large-sample delivery signals, so the diagnosis is robust regardless.
- Margin frame: contribution ≈ $55/order, so $53.49 CPA is near breakeven vs. the $28 target — urgent to fix, but not catastrophically bleeding.

### Smallest safe next actions
1. **Yes, launch 2–3 new creatives** — different angles/formats, not re-edits of UGC v3. This is the highest-leverage move because CTR is the bottleneck.
2. **Cap or reduce spend on UGC demo v3** to relieve the 72% concentration; let Founder story / Static benefit (CPA $46–49) take more share.
3. **Expand the audience** to cut frequency — add 1–2 lookalikes or broad/Advantage+ targeting. The 450k pool is too small for $2.3k/wk; that's the root of frequency 5.8.
4. Keep changes incremental so you don't reset learning across the whole account.

### What NOT to do yet
- Don't touch the landing page or offer — CVR is stable; that's not the problem.
- Don't slash total budget reactively or kill the campaign — the funnel works, the inputs are stale.
- Don't make many simultaneous changes (new creative + audience + bid + budget at once) — you'll lose attribution of what fixed it.

### Measure after the change
- **CTR recovery** on new creatives (target back toward ~1.8%) and their **CPA vs. $28**.
- **Frequency** trending down toward ~2–3/week after audience expansion.
- **CVR holding** at ~3.2–3.4% (confirms new creative quality isn't drawing worse traffic).
- New-creative purchase volume for **statistical significance** before scaling winners (don't judge on <15–20 purchases each).
