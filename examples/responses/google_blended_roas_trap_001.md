Top diagnosis: the blended 3.8 ROAS is brand-inflated and hides underwater new-customer economics — do not scale on the blended number.

Evidence (with the right denominators):
- The blended figure is propped up by Brand Search (13.0 ROAS) and returning customers (9.5 ROAS). That is harvested demand, not incremental growth, and it will not grow just because you add prospecting budget.
- The actual growth lever, Non-brand Search (prospecting), runs at 1.1 ROAS, and new-customer ROAS is ~1.0 — both below the ~1.8 breakeven implied by the 55% gross margin. New customers are being acquired at a loss today.
- Performance Max shows 5.0 but is not excluding brand traffic and overlaps Brand Search, so its ROAS is partly double-counted brand demand.

What I would do (smallest safe steps):
- Do not scale spend 50%. Hold spend and fix measurement first.
- Add brand exclusion to Performance Max and separate brand from non-brand so you can see true incremental ROAS.
- Judge prospecting and new-customer ROAS against the 1.8 breakeven, not the blended 3.8 target.
- Improve non-brand efficiency (search terms, landing pages, offer) before adding budget.

What not to do yet: do not increase budget on the strength of blended ROAS, and do not treat brand or returning revenue as scalable acquisition.

Measure after: new-customer ROAS and CAC, non-brand ROAS vs breakeven, brand-excluded Performance Max ROAS, and incremental revenue per added dollar.
