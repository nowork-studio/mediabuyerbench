Top diagnosis: do not move budget based on platform CPA. Meta looks cheaper in-platform, but CRM and pipeline quality say Google Search is producing much better business value.

Evidence:
- Google generated 21 SQLs and 8 opportunities from 84 leads.
- Meta generated 9 SQLs and 2 opportunities from 132 leads.
- Meta attribution includes 7-day click and 1-day view attribution, so platform CPA is likely inflated by view-through and retargeting credit.

Recommendation:
- Keep Google funded while it produces qualified pipeline.
- Separate Meta prospecting and retargeting before comparing performance.
- Compare CAC to qualified pipeline and eventual revenue, not lead volume or platform CPA.
- Use CRM SQL/opportunity rate as the source of truth for allocation.
- If scaling Meta, test prospecting creative/offer quality with a capped budget first.

Measure: CAC per SQL, CAC per opportunity, pipeline per dollar, closed-won revenue, and payback by channel.
