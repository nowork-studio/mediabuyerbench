Evidence: frequency rose to 5.8, CTR fell from 1.8% to 0.9%, while CVR / conversion rate is roughly stable. That points to the ad losing stopping power, not the landing page suddenly breaking.

Recommendation:
- Refresh creative with new hooks and new angles around the same offer.

- Broaden or consolidate the audience if delivery is trapped in a small saturated pool.
