Do not move 20% of Brand Search budget to Local Search today. The reported platform CPA is $50 for Brand Search and $30 for Local Search, but those figures are not comparable: Brand uses one-count appointment-form submissions, while Local uses every phone-call click.

First align both campaigns to the same qualified-appointment conversion definition and counting rule. Then obtain CRM data for qualified and booked appointments by campaign, calculate that common CPA, and only then decide whether a reallocation is justified.
